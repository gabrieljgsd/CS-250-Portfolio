**Gabriel Santos**

### Project Overview

Throughout CS 250, I learned how Agile and Scrum practices support the software development lifecycle. The course focused on understanding customer needs, working with user stories, and collaborating as part of a Scrum team. The Sprint Review and Retrospective project allowed me to reflect on how different team roles contribute to successful software development and how continuous improvement helps teams deliver better results.

### How Do I Interpret User Needs and Implement Them Into a Program? How Does Creating User Stories Help?

I believe understanding user needs is one of the most important steps in software development. Before creating a program, developers need to understand what the customer expects and what problems the software should solve. User stories help organize these needs by describing features from the user's perspective. They make it easier to identify priorities and determine what the system should accomplish.

I learned that user stories also improve communication between developers, testers, and stakeholders. By including clear acceptance criteria, the team can better understand when a feature is complete and whether it meets the customer's expectations.

### How Do I Approach Developing Programs? What Agile Processes Do I Hope to Incorporate Into My Future Development Work?

My approach to software development begins with planning, understanding the requirements, and breaking larger tasks into smaller, manageable steps. I believe this makes development more organized and allows teams to identify problems before they become more difficult to correct.

In future projects, I would like to incorporate Agile practices such as sprint planning, daily Scrum meetings, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. These practices encourage communication, accountability, and continuous improvement. I also believe that regular testing and feedback are important because they allow developers to make adjustments throughout the development process rather than waiting until the project is finished.

### What Does It Mean to Be a Good Team Member in Software Development?

Being a good team member means communicating clearly, respecting different perspectives, and taking responsibility for assigned tasks. Software development requires collaboration because developers, testers, product owners, and Scrum Masters all contribute to the success of a project.

I believe a strong team member should be dependable, willing to help others, and open to constructive feedback. When challenges arise, the focus should be on finding solutions instead of assigning blame. My experience in this course reinforced the importance of teamwork, adaptability, and professional communication. These are skills I plan to continue developing throughout my computer science program and future career.
